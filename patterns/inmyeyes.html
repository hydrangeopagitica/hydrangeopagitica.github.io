In My Eyes I Say

Translated by Olivia Schwartz from Miyazawa Kenji's 「眼にて云ふ」

All bad, isn’t it?
It doesn’t stop, ahh.
Because, ahh, it’s bubble bubble geysering.
Because evening-sent sleepless blood also pours continuously.
Thereabout it’s blue and so so silent
Whatever else, momentlessly I am death-bound.
But also what nice wind, isn’t it?
Already purelight is near, so
Like that, sky-sent, overflowing and geyser-like
Pristine wind comes, ahh.
Maple buds and fur-like flowers on which to,
Like on autumn grass, rise waves and
Burnscar-bearing rush-grass mats are also blue.
You’re on a medical conference’s return trip or something I don’t know but
Black frock coat draped,
So properly all kinds of treatment you bequeath me so
Of this to die even, really I’m regretless.
Blood-spurting aside,
That I’m this carefree and unagonized is because
My soul is half body-torn, I think.
It’s just that whatever else, blood withstanding,
That I can’t tell you is awful.
From your side this would seem a considerably pathetic vista I imagine, yet
From me what’s visible is
After all, the pristine blue sky and
The fathomlessly clear wind and nothing else.

Spring 2026, Oberlin
