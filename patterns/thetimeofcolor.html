The Time of Color

by Olivia Schwartz

Like friends and peers
You and I turn up our noses
We find the time to sneer

Incapacitated together
Taking only the counsels of roses
You and I turn up our noses
To lean over each other's nape
To leer
To pluck a bunch of grapes, to cull
Behind the other's ear

October 2025, The Second Circle
